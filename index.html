      A round is a time budget

      This is my suggested split. Do not spend all the time drawing boxes. Give the largest block to the hard part. End with failures and the release plan.

      • Scope and constraints4 min
      • Data and contracts10 min
      • Client structure8 min
      • Make the hard part work18 min
      • Break it, ship it, roll it back5 min

      Practise out loud

      Reading is half of the work. You also need to say your design out loud, in 45 minutes, and hear what is weak. A mock interview with a friend is a good way to do this, and these pages are free.
