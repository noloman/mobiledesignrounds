Pack waitlist

The study pack: does it exist? Not yet.

If you came here from a confirmation email: thank you, you are on the list. Nothing else is needed.

I am thinking about making a paid pack of the material on this site, for people who would rather study it in one offline bundle. It does not exist yet. There is no price, no checkout and no date. I want to find out first whether it is worth building.

What is planned

  • The case studies and guides as a PDF and an ePub, to read offline.
  • A cheat sheet for each level (mid-level, senior, staff).
  • Rubrics for scoring your own answers.
  • Decision templates, such as the architecture decision record from the native vs cross-platform guide.
  • A snippet repository with the Kotlin and Swift snippets, compile-checked where I could and marked where I could not.
  • A question bank for flashcards.
  • Bonus case studies that will not be on the site first.

This is a plan, not a promise. The list may change, or I may never make the pack.

What the waitlist means

  • You get an email if and when the pack launches. Nothing before that, apart from Kit's confirmation email.
  • There is no price and no commitment. Joining does not reserve anything and you do not have to buy.
  • The free pages stay free: every case study and guide on this site stays readable without signup or payment.
  • Double opt-in through Kit, and one-click unsubscribe. Privacy.

Join the waitlist

Your address goes to Kit, as a separate waitlist form. Unsubscribe anytime.

Optional: what would make this worth paying for, or what is missing from the list? Tell me in a GitHub issue (public; do not post your email address there).
