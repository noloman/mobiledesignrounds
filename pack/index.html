Pack waitlist

The study pack: does it exist? Not yet.

If you came here from a confirmation email: thank you, you are on the list. Nothing else is needed.

I am thinking about making a paid pack of the material on this site. It is for people who prefer to study in one offline bundle. It does not exist yet. There is no price, no checkout and no date. First I want to learn whether it is worth building.

Free right now: the practice kit

You do not need to join anything to start. These are free today and stay free:

  • The score sheet: score a mock round from 1 to 4 on six rows. Print it from your browser.
  • The mock interview script: run a 45-minute round with a friend.
  • The one-page cheat sheet: the five steps and a time plan.

The pack would add more: the same tools for each level, a full version of each, and offline files.

What is planned

  • The case studies and guides as a PDF and an ePub, to read offline.
  • A cheat sheet for each level (mid-level, senior, staff).
  • Scoring guides (rubrics) to score your own answers.
  • Decision templates, such as the architecture decision record from the native vs cross-platform guide.
  • A code repository with the Kotlin and Swift snippets. They are compile-checked where possible, and marked where they are not.
  • A set of questions for flashcards.
  • Bonus case studies that will not appear on the site first.

This is a plan, not a promise. The list may change, or I may never make the pack.

What the waitlist means

  • You get an email if the pack is released, and when it is released. You get nothing before that, except Kit's confirmation email.
  • There is no price and no commitment. Joining does not reserve anything and you do not have to buy.
  • The free pages stay free: every case study and guide on this site stays readable without signup or payment.
  • Double opt-in (you confirm by email) through Kit, and one-click unsubscribe. Privacy.

Join the waitlist

Your address goes to Kit, in a separate waitlist form. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Optional: What would make this worth paying for? What is missing from the list? Tell me in a GitHub issue. Issues are public, so do not post your email address there.
