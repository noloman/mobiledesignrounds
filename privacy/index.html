Privacy

Privacy

In short: this site sets no cookies, runs no analytics (tools that count visitors) and loads nothing from other websites. If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address goes to the email provider, Kit. Nothing else is collected.

What is collected

  • By this site: only the email address you choose to submit to the newsletter form.
  • If you subscribe: Kit (kit.com), the email provider, collects your email address when you submit the signup form. It is used only to send the newsletter. Kit handles it under its own privacy policy. Kit may also record technical data, such as when and from where a confirmation email or another email was opened.
  • The form is a plain HTML form. No script from Kit loads on this site, and this site sets no cookie. When you submit the form, your browser posts to the servers of Kit. Kit may set its own cookies on kit.com, for example for bot protection.
  • We use double opt-in. This means nothing is sent until you confirm. You can unsubscribe with the link in any email, and then nothing more is sent.
  • The same rules apply to the Pack waitlist form on /pack/. Its address also goes to Kit, as a separate Kit form. It is used only to email you if and when the pack launches.
  • To have your address deleted, ask through a GitHub issue. Do not post the address there. The author will reply with a private way to confirm. You can also use the data options that Kit offers.
  • By the host: GitHub Pages serves the site. As the hosting provider, GitHub may process the IP addresses of visitors and request details in server logs. GitHub's privacy statement describes this. This site does not receive that data.
  • If you open an issue or post in a GitHub Discussion to send a correction or a question, GitHub's terms and privacy statement apply. The issue is public.

Newsletter and Pack waitlist

The controller of both lists is the site author named on the About page. The controller is the person who decides how the data is used. The legal basis is your consent when you submit the form. You can withdraw your consent at any time by unsubscribing.

RSS

The RSS feed is a plain file. Your feed reader downloads it. This site does not track who subscribes.

If this changes

If analytics is added later, this page will be updated first. It will say what is collected, who collects it, and how to opt out.
