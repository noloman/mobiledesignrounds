Privacy

Privacy

The short version: this site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other websites. If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address goes to the email provider, Kit. Nothing else is collected.

What is collected

  • By this site: only the email address you choose to submit to the newsletter form.
  • If you subscribe: your email address is collected by Kit (kit.com), the email provider, when you submit the signup form, and is used only to send the newsletter. Kit processes it under its own privacy policy; Kit may also record technical data such as when and from where a confirmation or email was opened. The form is a plain HTML form: no script from Kit loads on this site and this site sets no cookie. When you submit it, your browser posts to the servers of Kit, which may set their own cookies on kit.com (for example for bot protection). We use double opt-in: nothing is sent until you confirm. Unsubscribe with the link in any email and nothing more is sent. The same applies to the Pack waitlist form on /pack/: its address also goes to Kit, as a separate Kit form, and is used only to email you if and when the pack launches. To have your address deleted, ask through a GitHub issue (do not post the address there) and the author will reply with a private way to confirm, or use the data options Kit offers.
  • By the host: the site is served by GitHub Pages. As the hosting provider, GitHub may process visitors' IP addresses and request details in server logs, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. This site does not receive that data.
  • If you open an issue or post in a GitHub Discussion to send a correction or a question, that is governed by GitHub's terms and privacy statement, and the issue is public.

Newsletter and Pack waitlist

The controller of both lists is the site author named on the About page. The legal basis is your consent when you submit the form; withdraw it any time by unsubscribing.

RSS

The RSS feed is a plain file. Your feed reader fetches it; this site does not track who subscribes.

If this changes

If analytics is added later, this page will be updated first and will say what is collected, by whom, and how to opt out.
