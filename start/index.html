Where to start

Pick the track closest to your level. Each is an ordered reading path through the case studies and guides, with the reason for each step, the reading time and what the page prepares you to say. The levels are the author's grouping, not an official scale; skip around freely. Everything is free to read, with no signup.

Mid-levelSeniorStaff

Mid-level track

Core client skills: local-first data, safe retries, paging and caching. About 95 minutes of reading in total.

  1. The framework4 minThe method every other page reuses, and where the 45 minutes go.You will be able to: walk through the five steps and say how a round's time could be split.
  2. Design a chat app15 minThe canonical offline example: outbox, idempotent sends, sync.You will be able to: explain why a send is saved locally first, and how a retry avoids a duplicate.
  3. Design a news feed14 minBuilds on the chat app: paging, optimistic writes, cache freshness.You will be able to: describe how a feed pages, updates optimistically and stays fresh.
  4. Design a pagination library15 minPaging as a reusable library: public API, generics, cancellation.You will be able to: design the public surface of a paging library and say what stays internal.
  5. Design an image loading library14 minCaches, decoding and cancellation behind a scrolling list.You will be able to: explain how an image loader keys, caches, downsamples and cancels requests.
  6. Design photo backup and file sync15 minResumable uploads, and who runs the transfer in the background.You will be able to: explain resumable block uploads and which system component schedules them.
  7. Design a hotel or ride booking app18 minHolds, server time and idempotent payments.You will be able to: describe a hold that expires on server time and a payment retry that cannot charge twice.

Senior track

Trade-offs and failure modes across sync-heavy and SDK-style questions. About 156 minutes of reading in total.

  1. The framework4 minThe method, and the time split to practise against.You will be able to: walk through the five steps and say where the time goes.
  2. Design a chat app15 minStart with the lost-acknowledgement walkthrough.You will be able to: explain the outbox and idempotency key, and what happens when an ack is lost.
  3. Offline-first mobile system design: one model, six case studies10 minThe one model behind six of the case studies.You will be able to: state the offline-first model and choose a conflict policy per kind of data.
  4. Design a news feed14 minOptimistic writes and cache invalidation under failure.You will be able to: defend a pagination and invalidation choice and its failure modes.
  5. Design photo backup and file sync15 minThe hardest sync problem here: blocks, conflicts, background limits.You will be able to: explain block-level sync, conflicts and platform background limits.
  6. Design a hotel or ride booking app18 minServer-authoritative time and payment recovery.You will be able to: explain holds, idempotent booking and how the app recovers after a crash.
  7. Design a stock ticker app14 minThe always-online contrast: streaming updates, conflation and resync.You will be able to: design a quote stream that drops stale ticks, detects gaps and resyncs.
  8. Design a video streaming app18 minPlayback state, prefetching and cost; builds on the feed and image loading pages.You will be able to: explain where the player lives, how far ahead to prefetch and what that costs.
  9. Design a push notification SDK20 minSDK design: token lifecycle, delivery and app states.You will be able to: explain token refresh, delivery limits and what changes in each app state.
  10. Design an analytics SDK19 minSDK design under constraints: batching, persistence, privacy.You will be able to: design an event queue that survives process death and respects consent.
  11. Staff-level mobile system design: what the round tests9 minOptional stretch: see where the bar moves at staff level.You will be able to: separate what a senior answer covers from what staff adds.

Staff track

Cross-cutting models, platform-level decisions and how to present them. About 160 minutes of reading in total.

  1. The framework4 minThe method, and the time split to practise against.You will be able to: walk through the five steps and say where the time goes.
  2. Design a chat app15 minThe worked example the staff guide builds on.You will be able to: explain the outbox and idempotent send before adding staff-level scope.
  3. Offline-first mobile system design: one model, six case studies10 minThe model that ties the case studies together.You will be able to: apply one offline-first model across chat, feed, upload, booking, analytics and push.
  4. Design photo backup and file sync15 minConflicts, versioning and background transfer limits.You will be able to: reason about conflict policy and transfer scheduling across both platforms.
  5. Design a stock ticker app14 minUsed in week 2 of the staff practice plan: cost, gaps and resync.You will be able to: reason about the cost of a feed and how a client detects and heals a gap.
  6. Design an analytics SDK19 minSDK ownership: versioning, remote config, multiple SDKs.You will be able to: talk about SDK versioning, privacy and rollout, not only the queue.
  7. Design a push notification SDK20 minPlatform differences, coexistence and failure testing.You will be able to: explain token lifecycle and coexistence with other push handlers.
  8. Design a hotel or ride booking app18 minUsed in week 4 of the staff practice plan: payments behind flags, modules, testing.You will be able to: explain how a payment-path change ships behind a flag and how the modules split across teams.
  9. Design a video streaming app18 minUsed in week 4 of the staff practice plan: player ownership, CDN cost, cohorts.You will be able to: weigh CDN bytes against engagement and say how player changes are compared between cohorts.
  10. Staff-level mobile system design: what the round tests9 minWhat staff answers add, and a four-week practice plan.You will be able to: say what changes from senior to staff and how you would practise it.
  11. Native vs cross-platform: write the decision record18 minEnds the track: a decision record for a framework bet.You will be able to: write a decision record with the assumption that would flip it, a migration path and a rollback.
